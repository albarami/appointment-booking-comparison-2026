## Who this guide is for (and not for)

**For you if:** you run a small clinic, salon, spa, therapy practice, or consulting business and need clients to book online — with reminders and ideally deposits — instead of living in phone calls, WhatsApp threads, or a broken free tool.

**Not for you if:** you need a full hospital EMR, a multi-location enterprise CPaaS suite, or only want a personal 1:1 meeting link with zero staff calendars (you may still skim the “solo consultant” scenario).

**Job-to-be-done:** pick a booking tool you can launch in days, that clients will actually use, without locking you into a dead end when you grow or leave.

## Scenario briefs

### 1) Solo consultant / coach
Prefer **Appointlet** or **Cal.com** if you mostly need 1:1 links, calendar sync, and light branding. Skip heavy salon POS suites.

### 2) 2–10 staff salon or clinic
Prefer **SimplyBook.me** or **FastBook** (and keep **Salonist** on a researching shortlist if you need POS+booking). Prioritize multi-staff calendars, SMS reminders, and deposits — use the criteria table cells, not affiliate payouts.

### 3) WordPress site already in place
**Simply Schedule Appointments** is the deliberate WP-native row. Budget plugin updates + hosting; export your appointments before you commit hard.

### 4) Already in Zoho / Squarespace
**Zoho Bookings** if you live in Zoho. **Acuity** if you are (or will be) on Squarespace — treat the $50 referral as a weak monetization signal for *us*, not as a reason for *you* to choose it.

## Pricing band notes (dated claims)

Treat every number as a **claim to re-check**:

- Free tiers often cap staff, SMS, or remove branding — fine for testing, painful at 8 chairs.
- WP licenses (SSA) look “one-time/annual” but still imply hosting and update work.
- Suite tools (Zoho) can be cheap *if* you already pay for the suite — expensive if Bookings is the only module you need.
- FastBook’s ~$19/mo headline excludes WhatsApp Automation / SMS / online processing extras called out on the pricing page.

## Migration & switching costs

1. **Export** client list + appointment history before you cancel the old tool.
2. **Re-book** active clients once with a clear “new booking link” message (email/SMS).
3. **Train** front-desk on the new calendar for one quiet weekday before peak days.
4. **Keep** the old link forwarding for 2–4 weeks if the vendor allows.

Lock-in is usually process lock-in (staff habits), not file formats — plan the habits.

## How to use this guide (checklist)

1. Circle your scenario (solo / multi-staff / WP / suite).
2. Shortlist **two** eligible tools using the **criteria table** (not the program table).
3. Open each product page linked above; start free/trial on both the same week; book a fake client end-to-end (reminder + deposit if you need deposits).
4. Pick the one your staff can run on a busy day — not the one with the prettiest homepage.
5. Only then look at annual vs monthly billing.
